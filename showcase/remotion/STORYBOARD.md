# Launch film storyboard

The film is staged as one continuous camera setup in a deep-navy space. One
phone and one tablet keep their world positions; quick 9–15-frame camera moves
reframe them, followed by still holds for reading and app footage. Cream type,
blue and peach path marks, and the original score stay consistent across the
94 seconds. Titles and source labels move on and off screen rather than fade.
A screen-content change is a source edit, not an uninterrupted real app
session. Labels distinguish recreated UI, physical OnePlus footage and
emulator/Test Store footage. Route lines are a story motif, not measured sync
data.

| Time | Story and motion | Source boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Giant “MOVIE NIGHT.” and “MILES APART.” reveal the two devices behind them as the camera approaches. | Editorial typography over illustrated app UI. |
| 3–19 s | The camera finds the phone for Start, then widens to include the tablet opening an invite and joining. The tap finishes before the joined state appears. | React recreation based on Flutter UI and captured test copy; labeled UI animation. |
| 19–35 s | Quick reframes settle with both screens visible for play, pause, seek and chat. Touches precede matching interface changes; the Bee image and playheads move while illustrated playback is active. | React recreation; no measured sync or runtime proof. |
| 35–52 s | The tablet travels upward out of view as the camera finds the phone for real Local Player Mode. Its frame expands in 15 frames for the separate landscape recording, then holds. | Physical OnePlus PLK110, Android 16, 1× capture; source switch is labeled. |
| 52–66 s | The frame contracts to portrait for Continue Watching; the camera then retreats as “ONE.” explains the daily free hosted session. | Android emulator capture, then labeled UI animation. Joining and reconnecting do not start a new session. |
| 66–80 s | Short camera moves settle on the full phone capture for Test Store purchase, same-customer restore and Glass Aurora. | Android emulator captures at 1×; RevenueCat Test Store, no charge. |
| 80–90 s | The same phone stage carries the reaction and another Plus session. | Android emulator captures at 1×. |
| 90–94 s | The camera sends the phone offstage; “TOGETHER.” and the Android/open-source sign-off slide into the open navy frame. | Editorial branding. |

Camera review points (30 fps): 60–75 intro approach; 330–345 tablet reframe;
570–585 paired playback; 870–885 chat; 1035–1050 tablet departure;
1050–1065 physical reframe; 1350–1365 landscape expansion; 1545–1560 portrait
return; 1980–1995 purchase; 2700–2715 phone departure.

The original direction draws on the storyboarding, animation and audio process
described by [Ordinary Folk](https://www.ordinaryfolk.co/process), the clarity
and dimensional motion of [Introducing Spline](https://www.ordinaryfolk.co/project/introducing-spline)
and [Webflow: A New Era of No-Code](https://www.ordinaryfolk.co/project/webflow-a-new-era-of-no-code),
and Remotion's [motion examples](https://www.remotion.dev/prompts). All film
code, composition and visual elements here are original; no template or
third-party footage from those references is included.
