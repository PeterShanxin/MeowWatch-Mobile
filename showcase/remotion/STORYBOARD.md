# Launch film storyboard

Restrained and product-first: a paper ground, one statement per screen, hard
cuts, and 3D motion only where it carries meaning — the two screens flying in
from apart and meeting in one room. Every app shot is real footage at 1× with
a source label.

| Time | Scene | Footage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5.5 s | The long-distance countdown in chat bubbles: “3, 2, 1, play!”, “wait”, “ok… again.” | Drawn. |
| 5.5–9 s | “Movie night, even miles apart.” and the MeowWatch mark. | Drawn. |
| 9–17 s | “Start a room.” then “Send the invite.” — a room starts and shows its QR invite. | Phone recording. |
| 17–23 s | “The movie comes along.” — an invite link opens, the join sheet names the video, and the room opens on it. | Phone recording. |
| 23–38 s | “Two screens. One movie night.” — MeowWatch for Windows and the mirrored phone fly in and meet. Play on the phone, pause on the laptop, skip ahead; both follow. | One real-time screen recording, two excerpts. |
| 38–46 s | “React together.” — hearts and popcorn sent from the phone float up on both screens. | One real-time screen recording. |
| 46–50 s | “Pick up where you left off.” — Continue Watching. | Phone recording. |
| 50–63 s | “Guests always join free.” / “Hosts get one free movie night a day.” then “More nights? Go Plus.” — paywall, Test Store purchase and a Plus theme. | Phone recording; RevenueCat Test Store, no charge. |
| 63–70 s | Mark, tagline, platform line and the Sintel credit. | Drawn. |
