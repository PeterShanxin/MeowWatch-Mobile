# Physical phone excerpts

These two app-only excerpts were captured from the ordinary `67d0206` Test Store
APK on a physical OnePlus PLK110, Android 16, on September 25, 2026. They show
Local Mode; they do not establish a paired session, purchase, or Nearby control.

- `physical-local-phone.mp4`: 10 seconds from 105–115 seconds of the local
  playback capture; portrait 880 × 1920.
- `physical-fullscreen-phone.mp4`: 7 seconds from 29–36 seconds of the landscape
  capture; landscape 1920 × 880, with the controls and system bars hidden.

Both run at their original speed. The phone encoded the captures; these bounded
excerpts are normalized to 30 fps for the film. No frame interpolation or
synthetic app state is used. Raw personal-device recordings are excluded.
